# Immersive Aircraft — Fly Rustic Airships Across Distant Biomes

Immersive Aircraft 9minecraft Mod brings a fleet of craftable flying machines to your skies. You can build rustic planes, copters, and giant airships to travel between distant bases. Every vehicle burns fuel, takes damage during rough landings, and holds inventory chests to transport your items across long distances.

Build functional flying vehicles using wood, iron, and basic materials. Check flight details with an instrument panel showing your speed, altitude, horizon, and compass heading.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Luke100000**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
